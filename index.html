GRAMMAR

💡 Grammar — The Passive Voice

Present simple passive — regular events 📅

Form: am / is / are + past participle (V3)

• The ceremony is held in the school hall every year.

• Souvenirs are given to all the guests.

Past & future passive 🕰️

Form: was / were + V3 (past) · will be + V3 (future)

• The first ASEAN Day was organised in 2020.

• The guests will be welcomed at the school gate.

💡 Use the passive when the action is more important than the person who does it — very common in formal writing: proposals, announcements and news.
🔁 Active: Students will hang the banner. → Passive: The banner will be hung by students.

STAGE 3 · READING

📖 Reading — A Proposal for ASEAN Day

Read the story, then answer the questions.

This year, Linh's school will host an ASEAN Day to welcome exchange students from other ASEAN countries. One morning, Linh sat in the school library and wrote a proposal for a welcome ceremony. In her proposal, she listed every activity: a traditional dance, a singing competition, and small souvenirs for all the guests. At lunch, she showed the proposal to Nam, and he loved the ideas. “A traditional dance will be performed, and a singing competition will be held,” he said happily.

The next day, Linh and Nam took the proposal to their teacher, Ms Hoa. She read it carefully and smiled: “Well done! Your proposal is approved. The ceremony will be organised by your class.” On Friday afternoon, the students prepared the school hall. A big banner was hung above the stage, and the flags of ASEAN countries were put in a row. Early on Saturday, chairs were arranged by the helpers, and everything was checked carefully.

At eight o'clock, the ASEAN guests arrived at the school gate. They were welcomed by Linh, Nam and their classmates with flowers and big smiles. In the hall, a traditional dance was performed by Linh's class, and songs were sung in different ASEAN languages. The audience clapped loudly after every performance. When the sun went down, a group photo was taken in front of the banner, and souvenirs were given to the guests. It was an ASEAN Day the students would never forget.

STAGE 3 · SPEAKING

🗣️ Speaking — Present your proposal

Work in pairs. Present your plan for a welcome event to your partner: the purpose, the activities and the times. Try to use passive sentences!

Useful language: Our event is called… · The ceremony will be held in… · The guests will be welcomed by… · A traditional dance will be performed… · Prizes will be given to… · Souvenirs will be given to all the guests. · We hope everyone will…

Good morning, everyone. Our event is the ASEAN Welcome Morning. It will be held in the school hall on Saturday. First, the guests will be welcomed at the school gate by our class. Then, a traditional fan dance will be performed on the stage. After that, a singing competition will be held, and prizes will be given to the winners. At the end, small souvenirs will be given to all the guests. We hope everyone will have a wonderful time. Thank you!

STAGE 3 · WRITING

✍️ Writing — A proposal for a welcome event

Write a proposal (120–150 words) for a welcome event for ASEAN exchange students at your school. Follow the frame below and use passive sentences.

Proposal frame:
📌 Title: A Proposal for …
🎯 Purpose: This proposal suggests … to welcome …
🕗 Activities: – 8:00: The guests are welcomed at … · – 8:30: A … is performed … · – 10:00: Prizes are given to …
✅ Conclusion: I hope this event will …

A Proposal for an ASEAN Welcome Morning
Purpose: This proposal suggests an ASEAN Welcome Morning to welcome exchange students from Thailand and Indonesia to our school next month. The event will help students learn about other ASEAN cultures and make new friends.
Activities: The event will be held in the school hall on Saturday morning. At 8:00, the guests will be welcomed at the school gate by our class. At 8:30, a traditional fan dance will be performed on the stage, and songs from different ASEAN countries will be sung. At 10:00, a friendly culture quiz will be held, and prizes will be given to the winners. Finally, small souvenirs will be given to all the guests.
Conclusion: I hope this welcome event will be a happy and useful experience for everyone.
